# Frequently Asked Questions

*Last updated: 10 May 2025*

**Can I return an item?**
Yes. You can return unopened items within 14 days. Bring the item and invoice to our store.

**Do you have a physical store?**
Yes, we have stores in Amman and Irbid. See the Stores document for opening hours.

**How do I track my order?**
Open "My Orders" in your account to see the status of each order.

**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Credit and debit cards, bank transfer, cash on delivery, and Pay in 4.

**Can I change the delivery address after ordering?**
Yes, while the order is still "Processing". Contact customer service.

**Do you sell refurbished products?**
No. NovaTech sells new products only.

# Price Match Promise

If you find the same product cheaper at an **authorised retailer in Jordan** within **7 days** of your purchase,
we refund the difference.

## Conditions

- Same model, same storage or colour, new and in stock.
- The competitor must be an authorised retailer with a physical store in Jordan.
- We do not match online marketplaces, second-hand sellers, flash sales, or bundle prices.
- One price match per product.

To claim, send a link or photo of the competitor's price to customer service with your order number.

# Pay in 4

Split your purchase into **4 equal payments**, with no interest.

- Available for orders between **100 JOD and 2,000 JOD**.
- The first payment is taken at checkout. The other three are taken every 2 weeks from the same card.
- A late payment fee of **3 JOD** applies to each missed payment.
- Pay in 4 cannot be used to buy gift cards.

Refunds and cancellations for Pay in 4 orders follow the Refunds document.

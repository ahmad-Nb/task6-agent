# Damaged, Defective on Arrival, or Wrong Items

## Report within 48 hours

If your order arrives damaged, does not work out of the box, or is not the item you ordered, report it within
**48 hours of delivery**. Use "My Orders" > "Report a problem", or call customer service.

Please include:

- Your order number.
- Clear photos of the item and the packaging.
- For wrong items: a photo of the product label.

## What happens next

1. We arrange a free courier pickup within 2 business days.
2. You choose a **replacement** or a **full refund**, including any delivery fee.
3. Replacements ship as soon as the pickup is done.

## Reported after 48 hours

Problems reported after 48 hours are handled under the normal returns policy or the warranty.

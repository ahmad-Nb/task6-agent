# NovaPoints Loyalty Program

Every customer with an account is a NovaPoints member.

## Earning points

You earn **1 point for every 1 JOD** spent on delivered orders. Points for returned items are removed.
100 points are worth 1 JOD of discount at checkout. Points expire 18 months after you earn them.

## Tiers

Your tier depends on your spending on delivered orders (minus returns) in the current **calendar year**.

| Tier | Spending in the calendar year | Benefits |
|------|-------------------------------|----------|
| Member | 0 – 499 JOD | Points on every order |
| Silver | 500 – 1,499 JOD | Points + free standard delivery on all orders |
| Gold | 1,500 – 2,999 JOD | Silver benefits + free express delivery in Amman + **45-day return window** |
| Platinum | 3,000 JOD and above | Gold benefits + **60-day return window** + priority warranty service |

Tiers are updated within 24 hours of a qualifying delivery and reset on 1 January.

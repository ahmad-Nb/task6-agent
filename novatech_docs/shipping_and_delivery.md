# Shipping and Delivery

NovaTech delivers **within Jordan only**. We do not ship internationally.

## Delivery times

| Area | Standard delivery |
|------|-------------------|
| Amman | 1–2 business days |
| Zarqa, Irbid, Salt, Madaba | 2–3 business days |
| Aqaba and all other areas | 3–5 business days |

Business days are Sunday to Thursday, excluding public holidays.

## Delivery fees

- Orders of **50 JOD or more**: free standard delivery everywhere in Jordan.
- Orders under 50 JOD: **3 JOD** in Amman, **5 JOD** in all other areas.

## Express delivery

Same-day express delivery is available **in Amman only**, for **5 JOD**, on orders placed before 2:00 PM on a
business day. Express orders placed after 2:00 PM arrive the next business day.

## Order status

- **Processing:** we are preparing your order.
- **Shipped:** your order is with the courier.
- **Delivered:** the courier has confirmed delivery.

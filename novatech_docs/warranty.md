# NovaTech Warranty

All new products sold by NovaTech include a NovaTech warranty. The warranty starts on the **delivery date**
(for store purchases: the purchase date).

## Warranty length by category

| Category | Warranty |
|----------|----------|
| Laptops | 24 months |
| TVs | 24 months |
| Phones | 12 months |
| Tablets | 12 months |
| Headphones | 12 months |
| Wearables | 12 months |
| Smart Home | 12 months |
| Accessories | 6 months |

## Batteries

Built-in batteries in laptops, phones, tablets and wearables are covered for **12 months only**, even when the
device itself has a longer warranty. A battery is considered faulty if it holds less than 70% of its original
capacity within the battery warranty period.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Physical damage (drops, cracked screens after delivery) and liquid damage.
- Damage from unofficial repairs or modifications.
- Normal wear such as scratches.

## How to make a claim

Bring the product and the invoice to the NovaTech Service Center in Amman, or request a courier pickup from
"My Orders". We repair the product within **10 business days**. If it cannot be repaired, we replace it with the
same or an equivalent model.

## Safety issues

If a product overheats, smokes, swells or smells of burning, stop using it immediately and unplug it.
Safety cases are prioritised and get a courier pickup within 24 hours, even when the warranty has expired.

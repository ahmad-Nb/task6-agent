# Privacy and Your Data

## Marketing messages

You can stop marketing emails at any time with the "Unsubscribe" link in any email, or in Account Settings >
Notifications. This takes effect within 48 hours.

## Deleting your account

Send a deletion request from Account Settings, or email privacy@novatech.example. We delete your account and
personal data within **30 days**.

We must keep invoices and payment records for **5 years** to meet Jordanian tax law. These records are kept
separately and are not used for marketing.

## Who to contact

Questions about your data: privacy@novatech.example.

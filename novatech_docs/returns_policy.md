# NovaTech Returns Policy

**Version 2.0 — effective 1 March 2026.** This version replaces Returns Policy version 1.3.

## Return window

You can return most products within **30 days of the delivery date**. The day of delivery counts as day 0.

Gold and Platinum members of NovaPoints get a longer return window. See the NovaPoints Loyalty Program document.

## Condition of returned items

- **Unopened items:** full refund, no fee.
- **Opened items that work correctly:** accepted if all accessories, cables and the original box are included.
  Opened laptops and tablets that are not defective have a **10% restocking fee**. No restocking fee
  applies to other categories.
- **Defective items:** handled under the warranty, or as a damaged item if reported within 48 hours of delivery.

## Items that cannot be returned

- In-ear headphones and earbuds once the hygiene seal is broken, unless they are defective.
  Over-ear and on-ear headphones are not affected by this rule.
- Software and digital licence codes once revealed.
- Gift cards.
- Items bought in a clearance sale marked "final sale".

## How to return an item

1. Open "My Orders" in your account and choose **Request a return**, or visit any NovaTech store.
2. For home pickup, a courier collects the item within 2 business days. Pickup is free.
3. We inspect the item within 3 business days after it reaches our warehouse.
4. After inspection, we issue the refund. Refund times depend on the payment method — see the Refunds document.
